## 3. Information the app processes

**What the site owner enters:** carousels — a name only the owner sees, the logo images with their alt text, optional
links and sizes, the optional heading, the layout, design and motion settings, and whether a carousel is live or a
draft. They are stored in the site's Wix collections. On the page, each carousel element keeps which carousel it shows
in the element's settings. The app reads the site's plan from Wix to know which features are included, and remembers
in its collections that the first-run sample carousel was already created.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. Visitors only see the logos; nothing a visitor
does — viewing, pausing, sliding or clicking a logo — is counted, stored or sent to us or anyone else.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it keeps a copy of the live carousel in
`sessionStorage` (keys starting with `lc-carousel-v1-`), so the logos appear at once on the next page of the same
visit; the browser deletes it when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether
a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own and loads no outside fonts. Logo images
added from the site's media are served by Wix. If the site owner adds a logo by pasting a link to an image on another
website, the visitor's browser loads that image from that website, which then receives the usual request data (such
as the visitor's IP address and browser type) under its own policy. The sample logos of the first-run carousel are
drawn by the app itself. A logo the owner links to another page opens that page only when the visitor clicks it.
Links in the dashboard lead to the app's page on the Wix App Market.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Carousels stay until the site owner deletes them in the app's dashboard (or in the site's CMS). Deleting a carousel
removes it from every page that showed it; removing a carousel element from a page keeps the carousel in the
dashboard. Image files added from the site's media stay in the site's media until the owner deletes them there.
Uninstalling the app stops the carousels from showing; a site owner who wants the carousels gone first deletes them
in the dashboard. A visitor can delete the stored copy at any time by closing the tab or clearing the site's data in
their browser.
