## 3. Information the app processes

**What the site owner enters:** the search box design, its texts and language, which kinds of content are searched,
pages to include or leave out, suggested searches, synonyms and pinned results. They are stored in the site's Wix
collections. The app reads the site's plan from Wix to know which features are included.

**The search index:** to answer searches, the app's server reads the site's **public** content — product names,
descriptions, prices, stock, categories, SKUs and images from the site's store, published blog posts (title, excerpt,
tags and text; for posts sold through a paid plan only title, excerpt and tags), and the text of the site's published
pages listed in its sitemap. Hidden products, unpublished posts and pages the owner left out or hid from search
engines are not read. This index is stored in the site's Wix collections and sent to visitors' browsers, because it
only holds what anyone can already see on the site.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. What a visitor types is searched inside their
own browser and is not sent to us, stored or counted. Opening a result simply opens that page of the site.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it keeps a copy of the site's search index and of the
search box settings in `localStorage` (keys starting with `site-search:`), so the search answers at once on the next
visit; the copy holds only the site's public content and is replaced when the site changes. In the site owner's
dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own and loads no outside fonts. Result images
are served by Wix. On sites on the free plan the search list shows a small "Powered by" line that links to the app's
page on the Wix App Market; it opens only when the visitor clicks it. Links in the dashboard lead to the app's page on
the Wix App Market.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

The settings stay until the site owner changes them in the app's dashboard. The search index is rebuilt from the
site's content whenever products, posts or pages change and at least every few hours, so a product or page the owner
deletes or hides leaves the index at the next update. Uninstalling the app stops the search box from showing. A
visitor can delete the stored copy at any time by clearing the site's data in their browser.
