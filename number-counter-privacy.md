## 3. Information the app processes

**What the site owner enters:** counter blocks — a name only the owner sees, the numbers with their signs, labels,
descriptions, start values, goals and icons, the design and animation settings, and whether a block is live or a
draft. They are stored in the site's Wix collections. On the page, each counter element keeps which block it shows
and its optional heading and short text in the element's settings. The app reads the site's plan from Wix to know
which features are included, and remembers in its collections that the first-run sample block was already created.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. Visitors only see the numbers; nothing a visitor
does is counted, stored or sent to us or anyone else. The app does not count visits.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it keeps a copy of the live counter block in
`sessionStorage` (keys starting with `nc-block-v1-`), so the numbers appear at once on the next page of the same
visit; the browser deletes it when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether
a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own and loads no outside fonts or files: the
number fonts are fonts already installed on the visitor's device, and the icons are drawn by the app itself. Links in
the dashboard lead to the app's page on the Wix App Market.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Counter blocks stay until the site owner deletes them in the app's dashboard (or in the site's CMS). Deleting a block
removes its numbers from every page that showed it; removing a counter element from a page keeps the block in the
dashboard. Uninstalling the app stops the counters from showing; a site owner who wants the blocks gone first deletes them in
the dashboard. A visitor can delete the stored copy at any
time by closing the tab or clearing the site's data in their browser.
