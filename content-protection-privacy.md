## 3. Information the app processes

**Settings chosen by the site owner:** which actions to block (right click, text copying, copy shortcuts, image
dragging, phone long-press, video downloads), whether text links keep their menu, the page rules (page addresses),
the notice text and the print and developer-tool options. They are stored in the parameters of the app's script in
the site's Wix settings. The app reads the site's address and plan from Wix to show the "Check my live site" link and
to know which features are included.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. The script runs in the visitor's browser with the
settings delivered with the page and only stops actions (it does not record them); nothing about the visitor is sent
to us or anyone else.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it stores nothing — with one exception for the site
owner: when the owner opens the site through the dashboard's "Check my live site" link, `sessionStorage` remembers for
that browser tab that the small check badge should stay on while the owner moves between pages; it is cleared when the
tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own and loads nothing from other servers on the
site.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Settings stay until the site owner changes them. Turning protection off removes the app's script from the site and
keeps the settings; uninstalling the app removes the script and its settings.
