## 3. Information the app processes

**What the site owner enters:** the business details for search engines (kind of business, name, description, logo
and photo links, phone, email, address, opening hours, social profile links, branches, return policy) and the blocks
added to pages (for example an FAQ, an event or a product). They are stored in the site's Wix collections, together
with a history of earlier versions. The form is first filled in from the site's business info in Wix; nothing is
written to the site until the owner presses Publish. The app reads the site's plan from Wix to know which features
are included.

**What the app writes to the site:** the published details become structured data (JSON-LD) in the SEO settings of
the site's pages, where search engines read them. These are public by design: the owner chooses what to publish. To
avoid duplicate data the app reads the site's page list and SEO settings, and reads the site's own published pages
the way a search engine sees them (the Check tab).

**Visitors:** the app collects **no data from visitors**. Nothing of the app runs in visitors' browsers; the markup is
part of the page as the site serves it.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies** and stores nothing in visitors' browsers. In the site owner's dashboard,
`localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** and loads no outside fonts. The markup uses the
public schema.org vocabulary. Links in the dashboard open Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema.org validator in
a new window for the page the owner picks; the app itself sends nothing to them. Other links lead to the app's page
on the Wix App Market.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

The details and blocks stay until the site owner changes or removes them in the app's dashboard. "Remove all markup"
takes everything the app added off the site's pages. Markup already published stays in the pages' SEO settings if the
app is uninstalled without removing it first, because the app then has no access to the site; the owner can remove
it in each page's SEO settings or by installing the app again and pressing "Remove all markup".
